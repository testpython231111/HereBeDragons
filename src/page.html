    How to play

    Every day the whole world gets the same seven prompts, like a country with red in its flag. Type a country or a city that fits. When the name is recognised, its flag appears. Press Lock in. You get one answer per prompt and 20 seconds.

    Any answer that fits counts. Your distance depends on how many other players named the same place: the rarer your right answer, the further off the beaten path you travel.

    Most prompts also hide one place, the dragon. The red line under the prompt is a clue that only a handful of right answers fit. Name the hidden one and you go Off the Map: +1,500 km. It's a gamble, because everyone sees the same clue and the places that fit it get crowded. Prompts with too many possible answers have no dragon.

      Names work in many languages (Norge, Deutschland, Moskva) and small typos are forgiven. Add the country to pick between cities with the same name: "Valencia, Venezuela".

      Before each day starts, the Machine (1,000 simulated players) takes the obvious answers and some of it chases the clue, so rarity works from the very first player. Real players' picks pile on top during the day.
